## Revisions to the original assumptions

- The targets for 40–60 articles, social followers and posts per week lack a business baseline. A local answer to an actual traveller question gives us something to test. Review the launch guides before commissioning more pages. [Google’s guidance on useful content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content)
- The homepage includes the AdSense publisher script and a separate interactive Ad Manager component. Neither source code nor a script count establishes filled impressions. Confirm actual earnings, approved display unit IDs and the regional consent setup before enabling manual units on long guides. Compare RPM, engagement and layout stability together. [Google publisher consent requirements](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/13554116)
- Directory income is a hypothesis until partners confirm bookings or renewals. Several public operator names repeat across service records. Review the stable IDs, services and contact details privately before combining any records. Count distinct verified operators and define the verification check.
- Confirm Google Business Profile eligibility before opening one. Ask whether staff meet customers in person during stated hours. The website alone does not establish eligibility. Keep X and Threads optional until a particular audience or partner justifies the time. [Google Business Profile eligibility](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036)
- The November 16–29 calendar includes both past and upcoming dates. Use it as a draft bank. Verify prices, legends, population figures, partner endorsements and footage rights before assigning dates.

Begin with one reporting or drafting schedule only after its data access and owner review time are known. [Scheduled tasks in ChatGPT](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/automations?surface=app)

## Monthly scorecard

Report search impressions, clicks, indexed guides, top landing pages, engaged sessions and the share of traffic from each source. Count call, WhatsApp and booking-link taps as intent. Count qualified enquiries and confirmed bookings only when there is separate operational evidence.

Page RPM equals estimated ad earnings divided by page views, multiplied by 1,000. Lead conversion equals confirmed bookings divided by qualified enquiries from the same cohort. Record currency, sample size and season. Compare the previous window and, once available, comparable weeks last year.
