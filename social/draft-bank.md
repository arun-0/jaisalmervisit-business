## Short copy starters

- **Itinerary:** “Planning two days in Jaisalmer? Give the city and the desert enough time. Start with our itinerary and check your transport and opening times before fixing the day.”
- **Safari:** “A dune transfer, a camel ride and an overnight stay are different bookings. Compare the full inclusions, pickup and cancellation terms—not just the headline price.”
- **Camp comparison:** “Choose the stay that suits your group. Ask about the bathroom, sleeping setup, nearby music, meals and return transport before paying.”

These starters need a final destination link, permitted asset, locally checked claims and publication review. Use only relevant hashtags and the actual channel's supported link placement; there is no universal posting quota.
