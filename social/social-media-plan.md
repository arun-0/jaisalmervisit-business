# Social media plan — Jaisalmer Visit

*14 Nov 2026. Starting point: Instagram 7 followers / 2 posts · YouTube 1 Short / 19 views · Facebook page new.*

## Is it worth it? Yes, for three reasons
1. **Jaisalmer sells itself visually.** Fort at sunset, dunes, havelis: travel reels in this niche get shared and saved a lot.
2. **Peak season is now (Oct–Mar).** People are planning trips right now. Start this week.
3. **Social helps the directory.** Camps, safaris and restaurants will pay to be featured by an account tourists follow.

## Priority and effort

| Channel | Priority | Weekly effort | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | 1 — main channel | 3 reels + 2 carousels + daily stories | Reach new travellers, send them to the site |
| **YouTube Shorts** | 2 — free extra reach | Re-upload the same reels | Shorts keep getting views for months; links to the site |
| **Facebook** | 3 — cross-post | Auto, via Meta Business Suite | Older Indian travellers, family-trip planners, travel groups |
| **WhatsApp Business** | Set up now, grows later | 10 min/day answering | Enquiries → bookings → commission |
| **Google Business Profile** | Do this week (free) | 15 min once | Shows up on Google Maps for "Jaisalmer travel guide" searches |

## The one rule: every post points somewhere useful
Each post ends with "Full guide → link in bio" (Instagram) or a direct link (Facebook/YouTube). Use a link-in-bio page or the site itself. This turns followers into website visitors, which turns into AdSense money.

## Content pillars (what to post about)
1. **Places:** one place per post, with the "what to actually look for" angle your site already has.
2. **Plan your trip:** itineraries, best time to visit, how many days, costs, what to pack.
3. **Myths and stories:** Kuldhara's legend, the "living fort", the brothers who built Nathmal Ki Haveli from opposite ends. These are the posts people share.
4. **Local picks:** featured camps, restaurants, guides from your directory. Free now, paid later.
5. **Tips and mistakes to avoid:** scams, fair auto prices, summer heat, dune timing. These get saved, and saves boost reach.

## Content production — simple setup
- **Footage:** phone video is enough. If you're in Jaisalmer, shoot 15–30 minutes a week: walking the fort lanes, sunset at Gadisar, dunes, food. Otherwise partner with the local reel photographers from your directory: credit them in exchange for footage.
- **Claude does:** hooks, scripts, on-screen text, captions, hashtags, the weekly calendar, replies to comments (you approve).
- **Editing:** CapCut or Instagram's own editor, using trending audio.
- **Posting:** schedule the whole week in one sitting in **Meta Business Suite** (it posts to Instagram and Facebook together).

## Growth tactics (free)
- **Collaborate with listed businesses.** Use Instagram's "Collab" post with a desert camp or restaurant, so it shows on both accounts. They get exposure; you get their followers.
- **Tag the location** ("Jaisalmer Fort", "Sam Sand Dunes") on every post, so people browsing that place find you.
- **Reply to every comment within a day,** and comment helpfully on other Jaisalmer and Rajasthan travel posts.
- **Repost travellers.** Ask visitors to tag @jaisalmervisit_, and reshare the best with credit.
- **Facebook groups:** answer Jaisalmer questions in Indian travel groups with genuinely helpful answers and a link to the relevant guide. Help first, no spam.
