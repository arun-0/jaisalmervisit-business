# What the Viator affiliate pilot means

This is a conceptual explanation. [Current approval/status](../TODO.md#approval-ledger) · [Exact owner steps/application/link plan](../setup/revenue-setup.md).

Viator is a marketplace for tours and activities. As an affiliate, Jaisalmer Visit would refer readers through a generated tracking link. The visitor would book on Viator, the supplier would deliver the activity, and an eligible completed booking could earn commission. Viator handles checkout/payments and booking customer-service questions in this link-based model. [How it works](https://partnerresources.viator.com/travel-content/)

```text
Useful guide → disclosed activity link → Viator booking
             → completed eligible experience → commission/payout
```

An ordinary phone/WhatsApp directory enquiry has no paid referral terms unless separately agreed. A click is not a booking.

## Economics and trade-offs

Viator advertises 8% commission, a 30-day attribution window and payment after experience completion. Bank transfers have a published US$50 threshold; confirm actual account/country eligibility and contract terms. [Programme](https://partnerresources.viator.com/). Cancelled bookings do not produce the same completed-booking earnings. [Creator guidance](https://partnerresources.viator.com/creator/)

If an eligible booking's commissionable amount were ₹5,000 at 8%, commission would be ₹400 before costs/taxes. This is arithmetic, not a guarantee for every checkout. Attribution, exclusions, deductions and payout conditions still apply.

| Advantage | Limitation |
|---|---|
| Simple links and no owner booking checkout | Visitor leaves our site; we depend on the marketplace. |
| Less handling than a WhatsApp booking desk | Supplier quality, availability and cancellations remain important. |
| Can earn on purchases rather than only ad views | Small traffic or poor fit can yield little or zero income. |

## Recommendation and audience fit

Use ordinary disclosed links first; no API or booking backend until needed. Recommend products for the traveller, not just commission. Be clear about open-air sleeping versus a camp with facilities, exact pickup location and what is included. Do not hard-code changing prices or ratings. Track completed outcomes and payouts separately from clicks.

For the selected [India-first market](../strategy/roadmap.md#audience-and-market), domestic checkout friction could outweigh the convenience of an international marketplace. The specific INR/payment/pickup/cancellation checks and unverified options are in [the setup pack](../setup/revenue-setup.md#india-first-review-gates). Compare an India-friendly alternative/direct partner if those checks fail, rather than scaling an unsuitable route.

The measured baseline and its limits are in [the audit](../reports/revenue-audit-2026-11-21.md); it does not justify a large immediate-income forecast. The approved direction is a pilot to learn what works with reasonable effort. Setup, legal/private owner actions and actual link candidates belong in the review pack rather than this explanation.
