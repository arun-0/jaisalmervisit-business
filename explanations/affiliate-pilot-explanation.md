# Affiliate pilot: what Viator is and why we would test it

Written for the owner on 21 November 2026. This explains a proposal; the owner has not yet approved programme signup or accepted its terms. The current work order is in [the roadmap](../strategy/roadmap.md#current-priority-todo-list).

## The idea in plain language

Viator is a marketplace for tours and activities. An affiliate is a publisher that refers a visitor through a special tracking link and can earn commission when an eligible booking results. Jaisalmer Visit would remain a guide and referral website.

For example, someone reading our camel-safari guide could choose “Check dates and prices on Viator”. The link would take them to a relevant safari listing. They would check availability and book there. The local tour supplier delivers the experience. With this link-based model, Viator is the merchant of record and handles booking payments and customer-service questions. [Viator's content-partner explanation](https://partnerresources.viator.com/travel-content/)

```text
Useful Jaisalmer guide
        ↓ visitor chooses a disclosed booking link
Matching activity on Viator
        ↓ eligible booking is tracked to our account
Visitor completes the experience
        ↓ commission becomes payable under programme terms
Jaisalmer Visit receives affiliate income
```

An ordinary operator phone/WhatsApp link on our site does not earn affiliate commission. No paid referral terms have been supplied for those contacts. A click or enquiry is not a completed booking.

## How the money works

Viator currently advertises 8% commission and a 30-day attribution window after a visitor uses the affiliate link. Commission is received after the experience is completed. Its published bank-transfer arrangement has a US$50 payout threshold; the actual payout route and eligibility must be confirmed for the owner's country/account. [Programme and payout information](https://partnerresources.viator.com/)

Illustration: if an eligible booking's commissionable amount is ₹5,000 and the applicable rate is 8%, the commission is ₹400 before our own costs or taxes. This is arithmetic, not a prediction or a promise that every ₹5,000 checkout produces ₹400. Confirm eligible amounts, attribution and deductions in the actual contract/dashboard.

Cancelled bookings do not produce the same completed-booking earnings. [Viator's creator explanation](https://partnerresources.viator.com/creator/) Tracking and payment remain subject to programme rules; a link click alone never guarantees credit.

## Why I suggest a small link-based pilot

For our current situation, the main advantage is low operating effort. We can test whether relevant readers become paying travellers without taking their money, managing availability or building a booking system. Viator describes ordinary affiliate links as a simple integration and states that signup and account maintenance have no subscription charge. [Link programme](https://partnerresources.viator.com/travel-content/links/)

Our observed audience is small: GA4 recorded 43 active users, 176 sessions and 403 views for 24 October–20 November, with consent and internal-testing limitations. That supports a practical learning pilot, not an expectation of substantial immediate income. We still need more qualified visitors and useful recommendations.

My recommendation is to use relevant text links or buttons first. Select experiences for the traveller's needs, not merely their commission. Compare a sunset outing with an overnight option where the page calls for those choices. Do not label open-air sleeping as a luxury tent camp, assume a Sam tour serves Khuri, or copy changing prices and ratings into static copy.

Add a nearby disclosure such as: “We may earn a commission if you book through these links. Check current prices, inclusions and cancellation terms on Viator.” Make clear that the traveller is leaving our site. Keep practical guide information useful even if they do not book.

Use programme-generated tracking links and a distinct campaign label per placement. The dashboard supports campaign tracking so we can see which links are useful. [Creating tracked links](https://partnerresources.viator.com/travel-content/links/create-links/) Measure outbound clicks, completed experiences, net commission and money received separately.

## Trade-offs

| Benefit | Cost or limitation |
|---|---|
| Existing booking/payment flow | Visitors leave our site and we depend on another platform. |
| Less owner handling than a WhatsApp booking desk | We have less control over the booking experience and supplier fulfilment. |
| Earnings tied to purchases | Revenue can be zero when traffic, attribution or completed bookings are insufficient. |
| Quick integration using links | Inventory, product suitability and link health still need review. |

A direct operator agreement may eventually earn more per booking, but sales, payment collection, refunds and reconciliation consume owner time. We have no agreed commercial terms to compare yet. Avoid creating both programmes and a booking desk before one route shows useful demand.

## What the owner would decide and supply

The next decision is whether to approve Viator as the first affiliate pilot. The owner would review the actual terms and supply legal payee/account type, country, contact and requested payment/tax details directly to Viator. Keep private documents and banking information out of chat and Git.

The application description and candidate activities are already prepared in [the revenue audit](../reports/revenue-audit-2026-11-21.md#affiliate-pilot-prepared-for-review). No account has been created, terms accepted or paid affiliate link published. AdSense identity verification is a separate process and does not have to finish before we prepare the affiliate route.
