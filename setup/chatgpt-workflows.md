```text
Optimise for additional net revenue with little recurring owner work.

three changes for Phase 1.

Use signed-in browser sessions and authorised tools first. Gather only
the account evidence needed to choose the actions. A recent complete
28-day view is enough to begin; use a prior period if readily available.
Continue independent work when a dashboard is unavailable and bundle
only the missing inputs needed for the selected actions.

Evaluate:
1. AdSense delivery and eligible-page coverage. The current homepage

   Inspect approval, settings, traffic and consent requirements before
   proposing Auto ads, manual units or a controlled experiment.
2. One relevant self-service booking route on up to three existing
   pages, using an approved affiliate programme or an existing partner
   with agreed terms. Check real inventory, attribution, completed
   booking commission and fulfilment responsibilities.
3. Small search/conversion fixes on the pages with the strongest
   evidence of commercial opportunity.

Count setup time, ongoing owner minutes, sales, support and reconciliation
in the ranking. Firebase billing is already handled.
Use the original Claude plan as the baseline and distinguish completed
seed recommendations from your additions or changed priorities.

Produce one concise ranked table with the evidence, proposed change,
revenue mechanism, honest upside range or unknown, setup work,
recurring owner time, required access/approval and review criterion.
Avoid unsupported revenue forecasts. Show assumptions where useful.

Prepare and test the smallest reversible implementation for the
highest-ranked feasible item. Make the publication or account-setting
approval concrete before requesting it. Honour existing authorisations.
Keep the phase within an assumed 15 minutes of weekly owner review,
and flag a chosen action that would exceed that assumption.

Save the plan and proposal in the existing business records and update
WORKLOG.md. Commit and push the planning changes. Stop with the
shortlist and tested proposal ready for my decision.

The later backlog includes broad social publishing, bulk content,
Hindi/channel expansion and custom automation. Bring one into Phase 1
only if the evidence shows a better return with similarly little work.
```

## Phase review

After implementation, record the exact shipped changes, metrics and review window. One short weekly review should cover revenue, relevant visits, commercial clicks, completed outcomes and owner time. A 14–28-day review is a checkpoint. Bookings and ad experiments may need longer or more traffic before a conclusion is justified.

Create a recurring automation only if the owner requests one after the manual view has proved useful. A schedule needs its own input access, run identifier, output and missing-data handling. [Scheduled tasks](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/automations?surface=app)
